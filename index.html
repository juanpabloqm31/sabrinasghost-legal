What Sabrina's Ghost Does

Sabrina's Ghost produces short, fast-paced videos covering true crime, mythology, paranormal stories, space, sports, and general trivia. New episodes are published regularly to YouTube Shorts, with the same content distributed to TikTok.

Publishing on TikTok

Sabrina's Ghost uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish video content directly to its TikTok account. This integration is used solely to post Sabrina's Ghost's own original videos — see our Privacy Policy for details on what data is involved.
