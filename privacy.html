Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sabrina's Ghost TikTok integration ("the App") handles information when Sabrina's Ghost publishes content to the TikTok platform.

1. What the App Does

Sabrina's Ghost is a short-form video content brand. The App is the publishing integration that uses the TikTok Content Posting API to upload and publish Sabrina's Ghost's own video content to its TikTok account.

2. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from TikTok viewers or other TikTok users. The only credentials handled are the OAuth tokens for the Sabrina's Ghost TikTok account itself, which authorize the App to publish content. These tokens are stored locally and are never transmitted to third parties.

3. Data Shared with Third Parties

No user data is shared with any third party. Video content is submitted directly to TikTok through their official API.

4. TikTok API Usage

The App requests the following TikTok API scopes:

  • user.info.basic — to identify the connected Sabrina's Ghost TikTok account.
  • video.upload — to upload video files to TikTok.
  • video.publish — to publish uploaded videos to the Sabrina's Ghost TikTok account.

Usage complies with the TikTok API Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be deleted at any time, which immediately revokes the App's access. No personal data about TikTok viewers is retained.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
