Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sabrina's Ghost TikTok integration ("the App") handles information when it interacts with the TikTok platform on behalf of its owner.

1. What the App Does

The App is a personal content-automation tool. It generates short-form videos and publishes them to a single TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API. It does not operate as a service open to the public and has no end-users other than its owner.

2. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from any TikTok user. The only credentials stored are the OAuth tokens for the single owner account, kept locally on the owner's computer and never transmitted to third parties.

3. Data Shared with Third Parties

No user data is shared with any third party. Video content is submitted directly to TikTok through their official API.

4. TikTok API Usage

The App uses the following TikTok API scope:

  • video.publish — to upload and publish videos to the owner's TikTok account.

Usage complies with the TikTok API Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention

No personal data is retained. OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be deleted at any time by removing the token file from the owner's machine.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
